## 1. Executive Summary & Core Philosophy

The **HireNest Intelligence Engine (HIE)** is the central, deterministic domain intelligence and decision-making platform for HireNest Enterprise OS. HIE decouples staffing business rules, scoring, risk assessment, recommendations, and forecasting from probabilistic Large Language Models (LLMs).

### The HIE Invariant Principles:
1. **Deterministic Primacy:** Business rules, scoring algorithms, compliance policies, and risk calculations are 100% deterministic, reproducible, and auditable.
2. **AI as Explanation Layer:** LLMs and AI Gateways consume structured, authoritative facts generated by HIE solely for natural-language presentation, summarizing, or interaction. No LLM computes business scores or policy outcomes directly.
3. **Event-Driven Reactivity:** HIE listens asynchronously to domain events via the `EventBus` and updates intelligence scores continuously.
4. **Algorithm Independence:** Scoring algorithms are abstracted via the `AlgorithmRegistry`, allowing tenant-level strategy overrides without breaking service interfaces.

## 2. Intelligence Scoring Formulas & Specifications

All scores produced by HIE range strictly from **0 to 100**. Confidence metrics range strictly from **0.00 to 1.00**.

### 2.1 Candidate Match Score ($S_{match}$)
Evaluates the fit between a Candidate ($C$) and a Requirement ($R$).

$$\text{Skill Similarity } (S_{skill}) = \frac{|\text{Candidate Skills} \cap \text{Required Skills}|}{|\text{Required Skills}|} \times 100$$

$$\text{Experience Score } (S_{exp}) = \min\left(100, \frac{\text{Candidate Years}}{\text{Required Years}} \times 100\right)$$

$$\text{Domain Overlap } (S_{dom}) = \begin{cases} 100 & \text{Exact Primary Domain Match} \\ 75 & \text{Adjacent Domain Match} \\ 40 & \text{General Tech Domain} \end{cases}$$

$$\text{Seniority Match } (S_{sen}) = 100 - (| \text{Level}_C - \text{Level}_R | \times 20)$$

**Overall Formula:**
$$S_{match} = (0.40 \times S_{skill}) + (0.25 \times S_{exp}) + (0.20 \times S_{dom}) + (0.15 \times S_{sen})$$

- **Interpretation:**
  - `90 - 100`: Elite Match (Immediate Technical Screen)
  - `75 - 89`: Strong Match (Proceed to Recruiter Review)
  - `60 - 74`: Moderate Match (Skill gap verification required)
  - `< 60`: Low Match (Auto-flagged or deprioritized)

---

### 2.2 Requirement Risk Score ($R_{req}$)
Assesses the probability of a requirement failing to fill or exceeding SLA timelines.

$$R_{req} = (0.35 \times \text{Budget Deviation Factor}) + (0.30 \times \text{Skill Rarity Factor}) + (0.20 \times \text{Client Feedback Latency}) + (0.15 \times \text{SLA Age Ratio})$$

- **Risk Levels:**
  - `0 - 25` $\rightarrow$ **LOW**
  - `26 - 55` $\rightarrow$ **MEDIUM**
  - `56 - 80` $\rightarrow$ **HIGH**
  - `81 - 100` $\rightarrow$ **CRITICAL** (Triggers Decision Engine escalation)

---

### 2.3 Vendor Quality & Reliability Score ($Q_{vendor}$)
Determines vendor trust tiers and automated bench routing preference.

$$Q_{vendor} = (0.35 \times \text{SLA Compliance Ratio}) + (0.30 \times \text{Submission Quality Index}) + (0.20 \times \text{Interview Conversion Rate}) + (0.15 \times \text{Turnaround Velocity})$$

- **Trust Tiers:**
  - `85 - 100` $\rightarrow$ **GOLD** (Priority routing & instant bench matching)
  - `70 - 84` $\rightarrow$ **SILVER** (Standard routing)
  - `50 - 69` $\rightarrow$ **BRONZE** (Manual recruiter review required)
  - `< 50` $\rightarrow$ **UNDER_REVIEW** (Restricted submission limits)

---

### 2.4 Recruiter Productivity & Velocity Score ($P_{recruiter}$)
Measures operational output and turnaround velocity.

$$P_{recruiter} = (0.40 \times \text{Conversion Rate}) + (0.30 \times \text{Response Velocity Index}) + (0.30 \times \text{Pipeline Throughput})$$

- **Efficiency Ratings:** `ELITE` ($\ge 90$), `HIGH` ($75-89$), `STANDARD` ($60-74$), `NEEDS_SUPPORT` ($< 60$).

---

### 2.5 Client Health & Attrition Index ($H_{client}$)
Assesses account health based on decision turnaround and placement consistency.

$$H_{client} = (0.40 \times \text{Feedback Velocity}) + (0.35 \times \text{Placement Consistency}) + (0.25 \times \text{Budget Realism})$$

## 3. Engine Architecture & Module Responsibilities

| Engine / Component | Primary Responsibility | Input Events / Data | Output Artifacts |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Matching Engine** | Skill similarity, experience alignment, seniority fit | `CandidateUploaded`, `JobPublished` | `CandidateEvaluationResult` |
| **Risk Engine** | Fulfillment risk, candidate drop risk, client attrition | `JobPublished`, `SLAExceeded` | `RequirementEvaluationResult` |
| **Recommendation Engine**| Contextual next-best-actions (NBA) | Entity metrics & risks | `RecommendationResult[]`, `NextBestActionResult` |
| **Forecast Engine** | Revenue, placement volume, pipeline probability | Active submissions, SLA stages | `ForecastResult` |
| **Knowledge Engine** | Ontology, skill graph, company relationships | Full-text & Graph queries | `KnowledgeQueryResult` |
| **Decision Engine** | Autonomous rule execution & workflow dispatch | High risk triggers, NBA events | Workflow actions & alerts |
| **Policy Adapter** | Candidate ownership, duplicate detection, SLA compliance | Candidate / Submission payloads | `PolicyEvaluationResult` |

## 4. Algorithm Versioning & Governance Policy

### 4.1 Metadata Standard
Every evaluation result emitted by HIE MUST include complete versioning metadata for auditability and trace tracking:

```json
{
  "score": 91,
  "confidence": 0.94,
  "algorithmVersion": "matching-v1.0",
  "policyVersion": "policy-v1.0",
  "knowledgeVersion": "graph-v1.0",
  "tenantVersion": "standard-v1.0",
  "traceId": "trace-1723012800000",
  "evaluatedAt": "2026-08-06T03:00:00.000Z"
}
```

### 4.2 Algorithm Lifecycle
1. **Experimental (`v0.x`)**: Internal sandbox testing.
2. **Active (`v1.0`)**: Production default.
3. **Deprecated (`v1.x-deprecated`)**: Retained for backward compatibility during 90-day grace periods.
4. **Archived**: Read-only historical replay support.

## 5. Policy Precedence & Decision Hierarchy

When deterministic outputs from HIE conflict, the system resolves them according to strict Policy Precedence:

```text
Layer 1: Security & Compliance Policies (Candidate Ownership, Duplicate Lock, Data Isolation)
  └── Layer 2: SLA & Business Rules (Rate Card Limits, Submission Thresholds)
       └── Layer 3: Deterministic Intelligence Scores (Risk, Quality, Match)
            └── Layer 4: Recruiter / Manager Overrides (Manual Recruiter Override)
                 └── Layer 5: AI Explanation & Natural Language Summaries
```

*Rule:* Recruiter manual overrides explicitly override Layer 3 and Layer 5, but CANNOT bypass Layer 1 (Security/Compliance).

## 8. Fallback Behaviors & Resiliency Controls

1. **Database Degradation / Timeout:** Fall back gracefully to `AlgorithmRegistry` default strategy heuristics.
2. **Missing Input Data:** Assign default neutral scores ($50/100$) with low confidence ($0.50$) and explicit missing field flags.
3. **Event Bus Disconnection:** Enqueue evaluation tasks in local memory buffer with retry backoff.

## 9. Required Telemetry & Audit Logs

Every evaluation call MUST emit structured OpenTelemetry spans and log events containing:
- `hie.operation`: Name of HIE method invoked
- `hie.algorithm_version`: Version string of algorithm used
- `hie.score`: Resulting score
- `hie.confidence`: Resulting confidence rating
- `hie.trace_id`: Unique execution identifier
- `hie.execution_time_ms`: Execution duration
